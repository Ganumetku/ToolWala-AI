## 5. Common Problems & Solutions
- **Problem 1: Photo has shadows on face or background**
  - *Why it fails*: Shadows distort biometric face matching.
  - *Solution*: Use front-facing window lighting, and stand a few feet away from the wall to avoid casting shadows behind you.

- **Problem 2: Glare on prescription glasses**
  - *Why it fails*: Eyes must be completely visible.
  - *Solution*: Remove your glasses before taking the photo. Most verification portals prefer photos without glasses to avoid reflections.
