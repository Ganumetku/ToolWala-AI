## 5. Common Problems & Solutions
Even with tools, applicants make minor mistakes that lead to rejections. Here are common problems and how to resolve them:

- **Problem 1: Shadows on Face or Background**
  - *Why it fails*: Shadows distort biometric markers.
  - *Solution*: Use diffused, front-facing lighting. Stand a few feet away from the wall to prevent shadows behind you, and use ToolWala's background remover to replace the backdrop.

- **Problem 2: Glare on Spectacles**
  - *Why it fails*: Eyes must be completely visible.
  - *Solution*: Remove your glasses. Even if you wear prescription lenses, passport offices prefer photos without them to avoid glare and lens tint rejections.

- **Problem 3: Red-Eye Effect**
  - *Why it fails*: Changes eye color metrics.
  - *Solution*: Avoid using the camera flash. Stick to natural window lighting.

- **Problem 4: Face occupies too little or too much of the frame**
  - *Why it fails*: Biometric templates require strict face-to-frame ratios (usually 70-80%).
  - *Solution*: Use the alignment grid in the ToolWala crop box to center your face between the guides.
