## 2. Why This Matters: The Reason Behind the 20KB Limit
For candidates applying to positions in Staff Selection Commission (SSC), railway recruitment boards (RRB), or state police departments, the 20KB limit can be confusing. Why do portals enforce such small file sizes?

### Technical Database Constraints
Government portals handle millions of applications during recruitment cycles. To process, load, and store this volume of records, systems enforce strict size limits. Keeping photos under 20KB saves server bandwidth and storage, and ensures the portal loads quickly even in rural areas with slower internet speeds.

### Biometric Verification Needs
While the file size must be small, biometric markers must still be legible. The system requires sharp contrast on eyes, nose, and facial borders. If your photo is compressed using basic tools, details blur, leading to application rejections.
