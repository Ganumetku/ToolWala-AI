01What is AI Resume Builder?

The ToolWala AI Resume Builder is designed to take the stress out of job applications. It's more than just a template—it's a smart assistant that helps you structure your career history for maximum impact. The tool ensures your resume follows modern design standards that are both pleasing to human recruiters and optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). It handles all the complex formatting, margins, and typography automatically, so you can focus on telling your story.
