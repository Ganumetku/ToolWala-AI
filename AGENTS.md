# Mandatory Humanized Tool Article Writing Standard
Always write and rewrite tool articles, introductions, feature explanations, and FAQs with a 100% human, conversational voice that passes AI detectors (ZeroGPT/GPTZero/CopyLeaks):
1. **Add Flow & Rhythm**: Vary sentence lengths naturally. Mix punchy short statements with smooth, relaxed sentences.
2. **Add Natural Human Imperfections & Pauses**: Use conversational punctuation (em-dashes, natural commas, occasional informal phrasing or candid side-notes).
3. **Use Everyday Conversational Language**: Avoid robotic marketing clichés and AI filler phrases. BANNED: "sophisticated design engine", "understands the nuances of", "delve into", "tapestry", "seamlessly blends", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "testament to", "elevate your", "embark on". Use words real people speak.
4. **Avoid Rigid, Sterile Structures**: Do not use predictable, repetitive sentence templates like "Whether you need X or Y, [Tool] does Z."
5. **Keep Word Count Concise**: Aim for ~80-100 words per section so it is quick to read, honest, and helpful without unnecessary fluff.
