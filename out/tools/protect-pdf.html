Protect PDF with Password

"Lock your PDF documents with secure passwords entirely in your browser. 100% private, fast, and free client-side encryption."

01What is Protect PDF with Password?

The ToolWala AI Protect PDF tool is a security utility that encrypts your PDF files and restricts access using a custom user password. Unlike standard web converters that upload your tax returns, corporate agreements, or financial statements to online servers (risking identity theft and security leaks), our tool runs 100% locally. The encryption process uses client-side javascript libraries to write a secure password lock into the PDF byte stream right in your browser. No file data leaves your machine, providing military-grade privacy.

02How to use Protect PDF with Password

1

Select a PDF document or drag and drop it into the secure upload deck.

2

Enter a secure password of your choice in the 'New Password' field.

3

Re-type the password in the 'Confirm Password' field to verify accuracy.

4

Monitor the strength indicator to ensure your password is secure.

5

Click 'Encrypt PDF' to lock the document, and download your password-protected file.

03Key Features

Browser-First Encryption

Your PDF is locked locally inside your browser memory. We never upload, see, or store your documents or passwords.

Password Strength Checker

Get real-time feedback on your password complexity to help prevent brute-force cracking attempts.

Show/Hide Password Control

Easily toggle the password visibility to make sure you didn't make a typo before locking the file.

Universal PDF Compatibility

The locked PDF outputs follow standard PDF security schemas, making them readable by any standard PDF viewer (Acrobat, Chrome, iOS Safari, etc.) that prompts for the user password.
