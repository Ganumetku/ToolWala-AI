01What is PDF Tools Suite?

PDFs are the backbone of modern documentation, but they can be notoriously difficult to edit. The ToolWala PDF Suite provides a set of powerful, lightweight utilities to manage your documents without needing expensive Acrobat subscriptions. Whether you need to combine multiple reports into a single file, extract a specific page, or shrink a large PDF for an email attachment, our tools handle it with zero friction. Most importantly, all processing happens locally using the `pdf-lib` engine, so your sensitive documents never leave your device.

02How to use PDF Tools Suite

1

Choose the specific tool you need (Merge, Split, Compress, etc.) from our PDF dashboard.

2

Upload your PDF files—you can select multiple files at once for merging.

3

Rearrange the page order by dragging and dropping the thumbnails into your preferred sequence.

4

Click the 'Process' button to let the local AI engine reorganize your document.

5

Download the finished PDF instantly—no waiting for server uploads or processing queues.
