01What is QR Code Generator?

The ToolWala QR Code Generator is more than just a simple encoding tool. It's a comprehensive design platform that allows you to create branded QR codes that people actually want to scan. From simple URLs and plain text to complex WiFi configurations and contact cards, we support all major data types. Our engine allows for deep customization, including custom color palettes, logo integration, and stylish frames with 'Scan Me' call-to-actions.
